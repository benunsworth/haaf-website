# HAAF Website — Volunteer Editor Handbook

Welcome to the Historic Army Aircraft Flight (HAAF) website management guide! This handbook is written in plain English for charity volunteers and trustees. You do **not** need any coding knowledge or technical background to update the website.

---

## 1. How the Website Works (The Big Picture)

* **Public Website:** Visitors go to **[haaf.uk](https://haaf.uk/)** to explore the aircraft fleet, view upcoming airshow appearances, read about our history, book the flight, donate, or send a message.
* **Volunteer Admin Area:** You update the website by visiting **[haaf.uk/admin](https://haaf.uk/admin)** in any web browser on your computer, tablet, or phone.
* **Automatic Publishing:** Whenever you make a change in the admin area and click **Publish**, the website updates automatically in about 30 seconds.

---

## 2. Logging In for the First Time

1. You will receive an email invitation from **Netlify / HAAF Website** with a link to accept the invitation.
2. Click the link and choose a secure password.
3. Once your account is active, go to:
   👉 **`https://haaf.uk/admin`**
4. Enter your email and password (or use the magic login link sent to your inbox).
5. You are now in the HAAF Content Manager!

---

## 3. Adding or Updating Upcoming Events & Airshows

Whenever the Flight confirms an appearance at an airshow, flypast, or hangar tour:

1. In the left-hand sidebar of `/admin`, click on **"Appearances & Events"**.
2. To edit an existing event: click its title in the list.
3. To add a new event: click the **"New Event"** button at the top.
4. Fill in the simple form fields:
   * **Event Title:** (e.g. *Midlands Air Festival 2027*)
   * **Date:** (e.g. *30 May – 1 June 2027*)
   * **Location / Venue:** (e.g. *Ragley Hall, Warwickshire*)
   * **Aircraft Attending:** (e.g. *Sioux AH.1 & Auster AOP.9*)
   * **Status:** Choose *Confirmed*, *Provisional*, or *Completed*.
   * **Description / Notes:** Add any visitor details or display times.
   * **Ticket / Info Link:** Optional link to the event's official website.
5. Click the green **Save** button, then click **Publish**.
6. The event will appear on the live website within a minute!

---

## 4. Updating General Website Information

To edit text such as the Welcome message, Our Story, STEM details, or charity notices:

1. In the left-hand sidebar, click on **"Page Content"**.
2. Click on **"Home Page Sections"**.
3. You will see clearly labeled boxes for each section of the site:
   * **Hero Title & Subtitle**
   * **About Narrative**
   * **STEM & Education Details**
   * **Charity Registration Number**
4. Make your text edits just like in Microsoft Word or an email.
5. Click **Save** and **Publish**.

---

## 5. What Happens When Someone Fills Out a Form?

There are two inquiry forms on the website:
1. **Request an Aircraft Appearance:** Used by airshow organizers, memorial planners, and event coordinators requesting a flying or static appearance.
2. **General / STEM Contact Form:** Used by schools, cadets, visitors, and general public inquiries.

* When a visitor submits either form, the inquiry is automatically forwarded directly to **`info@haaf.uk`** (your charity Gmail).
* The form has built-in invisible spam filtering, so spam bots are blocked before reaching your inbox.
* You reply to the visitor simply by hitting "Reply" in Gmail!

---

## 6. The Online Shop & Donations

* **Donations:** Visitors can select a suggested amount (£10, £25, £50, £100, or custom) and are directed to our verified donation channel.
* **Shop:** Merchandise (such as challenge coins, trolley coins, and apparel) links out to the dedicated SumUp store. If the shop web address ever changes, it can be updated in `/admin` under **Page Content** -> **Shop Link URL**.
