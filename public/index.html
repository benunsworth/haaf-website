VETERANS · REMEMBRANCE · COMMUNITY

Aircraft Appearances & Events

Bringing British Army aviation heritage to commemorations, veterans' gatherings and public events.

Veterans & Remembrance

We welcome enquiries for regimental reunions, veterans' gatherings, remembrance occasions, Royal British Legion and Poppy Appeal events. Our aircraft help tell the stories of those who served.

Participation is considered individually; no organisational endorsement is implied.

Flypasts & Special Occasions

Enquire about military funeral and memorial flypasts, airshows, weddings and other appropriate events. Each request is assessed for aircraft and crew availability, operational feasibility and required permissions.

Photograph: HAAF Beaver XP820 on the ground; illustrative of the aircraft, not a flypast.

Skeeter — Tow-in Static Display

Our non-flying Skeeter is available for consideration as a transported static exhibit at suitable village shows, veterans' gatherings, Poppy Appeals and community events.

Historic flying photograph; HAAF's display aircraft is non-flying.

Gazelle — Tow-in Static Display

Our non-flying Gazelle can be brought to suitable events as a static display, subject to transport, access, site suitability and volunteer availability.

Photo: Dave Taskis / Helis.com (credit visible on supplied image; reuse permission to confirm).
Planning an appearance? Please contact us early. Enquiries do not constitute confirmed bookings. All appearances depend on feasibility, transport or flying arrangements, insurance and necessary authorisations. STEM and educational visits are handled separately on our STEM & Education page.

INSPIRING THE NEXT GENERATION

STEM & Education

Explore science, technology, engineering and mathematics through the aircraft and people of HAAF.

Historic aircraft provide a unique opportunity to see engineering in the real world. We welcome expressions of interest from students, teachers, colleges and cadet leaders who want to explore aviation and engineering. All proposed visits and activities are subject to availability, safeguarding arrangements and site approval.

⚙

Future Aircraft Engineers

Thinking about a career in engineering? Discover the different skills needed to inspect, maintain and preserve aircraft, and learn about possible training, apprenticeship and career routes. Ask us about suitable opportunities to meet volunteer engineers.

ASK ABOUT ENGINEERING →

School Visits

Introduce pupils to flight, forces, materials, teamwork and aviation history. Potential activities include aircraft familiarisation, questions with engineers and age-appropriate practical demonstrations, tailored to what HAAF can safely offer.
